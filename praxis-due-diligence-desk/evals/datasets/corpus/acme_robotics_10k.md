# Acme Robotics, Inc. — Annual Report (FY2025 10-K extract)

## Financial highlights

Acme Robotics reported total revenue of $42.0 million for fiscal year 2025, up
from $32.3 million in FY2024 — growth of 30% year over year. Gross margin was
58%, up two points on improved contract-manufacturing terms. The company remains
unprofitable: net loss for the year was $11.4 million, an improvement from a
$14.1 million loss in FY2024. Cash and equivalents were $61 million at year end,
giving roughly 20 months of runway at the current burn rate.

## Revenue concentration

Two customers accounted for 41% of FY2025 revenue (Customer A: 24%, Customer B:
17%). The loss of either would materially affect results.

## Risk factors

The most significant operational risk is supplier concentration: 78% of Acme's
precision actuators are sourced from a single vendor with manufacturing located
in Taiwan. A disruption there — geopolitical, natural disaster, or capacity —
would halt production. Management began qualifying a second US-based supplier in
Q3 FY2025 but does not expect meaningful volume before late FY2026.

Other risks: the company faces litigation from Nimbus Systems alleging patent
infringement on gripper actuation (filed March 2025, unresolved). A negative
outcome could require design changes or licensing payments.
