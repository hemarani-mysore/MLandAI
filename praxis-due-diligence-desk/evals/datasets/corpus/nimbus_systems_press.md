# Nimbus Systems — Press & Legal Tracker (2025)

Nimbus Systems is a robotics manipulation startup based in Boston, founded 2020
by two former Toyota Research Institute engineers, Hannah Cole and David Reyes.

## Funding

Nimbus raised a $28 million Series A in 2023 led by Lux Capital. No later round
has been announced. The company is estimated at 30–40 employees.

## Litigation

In March 2025 Nimbus filed a patent-infringement suit against Acme Robotics in
the District of Delaware, alleging Acme's gripper actuation infringes Nimbus
patent US 11,xxx,xxx. Nimbus seeks an injunction and damages. Acme has called
the suit "meritless." The case is at an early stage with no trial date set.
Analysts view it as a competitive-pressure tactic more than a strong claim.

## Product

Nimbus sells a bin-picking cell for e-commerce fulfillment. Reported customers
include two regional 3PL operators. Nimbus does not disclose revenue.
