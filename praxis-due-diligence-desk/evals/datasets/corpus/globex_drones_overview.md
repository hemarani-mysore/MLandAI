# Globex Corp — Company Overview (industry database entry, 2025)

Globex Corp builds autonomous warehouse inventory drones. Founded 2017,
headquartered in Rotterdam.

## Funding

Globex raised a $120 million Series C in June 2024, led by Sequoia Capital, with
participation from existing investors Atomico and Prologis Ventures. Total raised
to date is $196 million. The round valued the company at approximately $850
million post-money. Globex has stated the Series C funds international expansion
and does not expect to raise again before 2027.

## Market

Globex operates in the warehouse-automation segment, competing with Dexory and
Verity. Its differentiation is a docking-and-charging system that allows drones
to run 22 hours a day unattended. Globex reported 40 deployed customer sites at
the end of 2024, up from 11 a year earlier.

## Relationship to Acme Robotics

None. Globex uses fixed-wing and quadrotor airframes and does not build
manipulators; the two companies do not compete.
