# Tundra Robotics — Company Overview

Tundra Robotics builds autonomous pallet-moving robots for warehouse
logistics, deployed alongside human workers rather than requiring a fully
automated facility redesign.

**Company facts.** Founded 2021. Raised a $20M Series A. 45 employees.

**Traction.** 14 deployed customer sites. $4.5M ARR, growing 80% year over
year.

**Product & IP.** Three patents pending on autonomous-navigation and
obstacle-avoidance methods for mixed human/robot warehouse floors.

**Market & competition.** Competes with Locus Robotics and 6 River Systems,
both larger and better-funded; Tundra differentiates on lower upfront hardware
cost and a faster (3-week) deployment timeline versus a typical 8-12 week
industry-standard rollout.

**Supply chain.** All of Tundra's robots use a single depth-sensor vendor for
obstacle detection; the company has not yet qualified a second sensor
supplier.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** Single-vendor dependency for a
safety-critical hardware component (depth sensors), with no qualified
secondary supplier in place.
