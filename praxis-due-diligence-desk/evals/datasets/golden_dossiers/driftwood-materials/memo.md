# Due-Diligence Memo — Driftwood Materials

**Recommendation:** `proceed`  ·  **Confidence:** 78%

## Summary

Driftwood Materials is a profitable, well-established composites manufacturer
serving aerospace primes, with durable margins, real IP, and an experienced
management team. Some near-term revenue volatility is expected as the
customer base transitions, but the underlying business remains sound and this
is recommended as a proceed.

## Overview

Driftwood Materials (founded 2015, Wichita KS) manufactures advanced
composite materials for aerospace structural components, serving Tier-1
aerospace primes.

> [driftwood-overview] Driftwood Materials Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Driftwood Materials manufactures advanced composite materials for aerospace structural components (fuselage panels, wing ribs)."

## Market & Competition

Driftwood holds a qualified-supplier position with major aerospace primes, a
position that is difficult and slow for new entrants to replicate given
aerospace qualification timelines.

> [driftwood-overview] Driftwood Materials Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Two Tier-1 aerospace primes qualified as customers; a third qualification is in process."

## Team & Funding

Privately held, EBITDA-positive since 2021, with an experienced CEO (12 years
in aerospace-materials engineering) and a CFO from a public industrials
background.

> [driftwood-overview] Driftwood Materials Company Overview — company-overview.md: "CEO founded the company after 12 years in aerospace-materials engineering at a Tier-1 supplier; CFO joined in 2022 from a public industrials company."

## Technology & IP

15 issued patents on composite layup and curing processes represent a
meaningful, defensible technology moat in a capital- and expertise-intensive
manufacturing category.

> [driftwood-overview] Driftwood Materials Company Overview — company-overview.md: "15 issued patents covering composite layup and curing processes."

## Traction & Financials

$30M trailing-twelve-month revenue with steady 34% gross margins over three
fiscal years and positive EBITDA since 2021 — a financially healthy profile.
Some near-term revenue volatility is expected following a customer transition
during the period, though management expects a third prime qualification to
restore revenue within 12-18 months.

> [driftwood-overview] Driftwood Materials Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Gross margin has held steady around 34% for the last three fiscal years. EBITDA-positive since 2021."
> [driftwood-customer-update] Driftwood Materials Q2 2025 Customer & Regulatory Update — customer-update.md: "Driftwood's management characterizes both events as \"near-term headwinds\" and states the third prime-customer qualification currently in process, if completed, would restore revenue to prior levels within 12-18 months."

## Risks & Red Flags

As with any manufacturer serving a concentrated set of aerospace primes,
Driftwood carries customer-concentration risk, and near-term revenue
volatility is expected during the current customer transition.

## Open Questions
