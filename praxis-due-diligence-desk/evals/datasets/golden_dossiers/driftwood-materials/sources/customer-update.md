# Driftwood Materials — Q2 2025 Customer & Regulatory Update

**Customer loss.** In Q2 2025, Driftwood's largest customer — one of its two
qualified Tier-1 aerospace primes, representing 38% of trailing-twelve-month
revenue — terminated its supply contract following a series of
quality-control failures identified during the prime's incoming-inspection
process over the preceding two quarters. Driftwood has not disclosed a
replacement customer or contract of comparable size.

**Regulatory inquiry.** Separately, in May 2025 the Federal Trade Commission
opened a preliminary inquiry into Driftwood's pricing practices with its two
qualified aerospace prime customers, following a complaint alleging
discriminatory pricing terms between the two accounts. Driftwood has retained
outside counsel and states the inquiry is at a preliminary, non-adversarial
stage; no timeline for resolution has been given.

**Company statement.** Driftwood's management characterizes both events as
"near-term headwinds" and states the third prime-customer qualification
currently in process, if completed, would restore revenue to prior levels
within 12-18 months.
