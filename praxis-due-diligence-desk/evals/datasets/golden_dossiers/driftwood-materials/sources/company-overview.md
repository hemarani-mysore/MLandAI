# Driftwood Materials — Company Overview

Driftwood Materials manufactures advanced composite materials for aerospace
structural components (fuselage panels, wing ribs).

**Company facts.** Founded 2015, Wichita KS. $30M trailing-twelve-month
revenue, 200 employees, privately held with two institutional investors on
the board.

**Product & IP.** 15 issued patents covering composite layup and curing
processes. Two Tier-1 aerospace primes qualified as customers; a third
qualification is in process.

**Financials.** Gross margin has held steady around 34% for the last three
fiscal years. EBITDA-positive since 2021.

**Team.** CEO founded the company after 12 years in aerospace-materials
engineering at a Tier-1 supplier; CFO joined in 2022 from a public
industrials company.
