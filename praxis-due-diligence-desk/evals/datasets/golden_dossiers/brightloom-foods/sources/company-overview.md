# Brightloom Foods — Company Overview

Brightloom Foods manufactures plant-based protein ingredients using a
proprietary fermentation process, sold B2B to food manufacturers and grocery
private-label programs.

**Company facts.** Founded 2018, Denver CO. $22M raised across seed and
Series A. 60 employees, single manufacturing facility in Colorado.

**Traction.** $9M trailing-twelve-month revenue, growing 28% year over year —
down from 52% growth the prior year, a deceleration the company attributes to
one large retail customer normalizing order volumes after an initial
stocking bulge. Three major regional grocery chains as direct customers;
gross margin is 34%.

**Product & IP.** Two issued patents on the core fermentation process. All
production currently runs through the single Colorado facility; a second
facility is in early site-selection stages but unfunded.

**Team.** CEO and CTO are co-founders with backgrounds in food science and
industrial fermentation, respectively.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** Single-facility production
concentration (no manufacturing redundancy if the facility is disrupted);
year-over-year growth deceleration from 52% to 28%.
