# Cascade Fintech — Company Overview

Cascade Fintech provides a B2B payments infrastructure API that lets SMB
marketplaces embed payment processing, split payouts, and compliance
workflows without building them in-house.

**Company facts.** Founded 2020, Series A ($12M raised total), 25 employees.
Two co-founders, no dedicated compliance officer as of this writing.

**Traction.** $340M in annualized processing volume, at a 0.4% take rate —
approximately $1.4M in annualized revenue. Growing 90% year over year off a
small base. Two marketplace-platform integrations are live in production;
three more are in the partnership pipeline.

**Compliance & security.** SOC2 Type II certified. Payments-compliance
responsibilities (KYC/AML monitoring, dispute handling) are currently split
between the two co-founders and an outside compliance-consulting firm on
retainer; the company has not yet hired a dedicated in-house compliance
officer despite handling third-party payment flows at meaningful volume.

**Team.** Both co-founders have prior fintech engineering backgrounds; neither
has direct payments-compliance or regulatory experience.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** No dedicated in-house compliance
officer yet, despite processing $340M in annualized third-party payment
volume — currently covered by an outside consulting retainer as an interim
measure.
