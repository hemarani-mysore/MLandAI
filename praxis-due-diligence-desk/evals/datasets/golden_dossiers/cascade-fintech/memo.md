# Due-Diligence Memo — Cascade Fintech

**Recommendation:** `proceed_with_conditions`  ·  **Confidence:** 68%

## Summary

Cascade is an early-stage payments-infrastructure API with strong relative
growth (90% YoY) and a real processing-volume base ($340M annualized), but the
absence of a dedicated in-house compliance officer is a material gap for a
company handling third-party payment flows at this scale, and should be
resolved as a condition of proceeding.

## Overview

Cascade Fintech (founded 2020) provides a B2B payments infrastructure API
letting SMB marketplaces embed payment processing, split payouts, and
compliance workflows.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "Cascade Fintech provides a B2B payments infrastructure API that lets SMB marketplaces embed payment processing, split payouts, and compliance workflows without building them in-house."

## Market & Competition

Two marketplace-platform integrations are already live in production with
three more in the pipeline, evidence of real distribution traction rather
than a product still seeking its first design partners.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "Two marketplace-platform integrations are live in production; three more are in the partnership pipeline."

## Team & Funding

$12M raised total through a Series A. Both co-founders bring fintech
engineering backgrounds but neither has direct payments-compliance or
regulatory experience — relevant context for the compliance gap noted below.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "Both co-founders have prior fintech engineering backgrounds; neither has direct payments-compliance or regulatory experience."

## Technology & IP

SOC2 Type II certification is in place, a baseline requirement for handling
third-party payment data, though the source material does not describe any
proprietary technology or IP beyond the certification.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "SOC2 Type II certified."

## Traction & Financials

$340M in annualized processing volume at a 0.4% take rate (~$1.4M annualized
revenue), growing 90% YoY off a small base — a real but early-stage revenue
picture where the growth rate should be read in light of the small starting
base.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "$340M in annualized processing volume, at a 0.4% take rate — approximately $1.4M in annualized revenue. Growing 90% year over year off a small base."

## Risks & Red Flags

Cascade has no dedicated in-house compliance officer despite processing $340M
in annualized third-party payment volume; this is currently covered only by
an outside consulting retainer and two co-founders without direct
payments-compliance experience. For a company in this specific category, this
is a real gap, not a minor administrative one, and should be closed before
volume scales further.

> [cascade-overview] Cascade Fintech Company Overview — overview.md: "No dedicated in-house compliance officer yet, despite processing $340M in annualized third-party payment volume — currently covered by an outside consulting retainer as an interim measure."

## Open Questions

- What is the hiring timeline for a dedicated compliance officer?
- What track record does the outside compliance consultancy have with comparable-scale payment platforms?
