# Due-Diligence Memo — Solace Health Analytics

**Recommendation:** `proceed_with_conditions`  ·  **Confidence:** 75%

## Summary

Solace is a Series C claims-analytics vendor for mid-size health payers with
strong, consistent metrics across growth (40% YoY), retention (118% NRR), and
differentiated IP (three issued patents). The recommendation is conditioned
on the two named risks — 9-14 month sales cycles and 35% of pipeline running
through two systems-integrator partners — which are disclosed plainly at the
scale the sources describe, not minimized.

## Overview

Solace Health Analytics (founded 2017, Minneapolis MN) sells claims-anomaly
detection SaaS to mid-size health insurance payers, catching anomalous billing
patterns pre-payment.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Solace Health Analytics builds claims-analytics SaaS for mid-size health insurance payers, focused on detecting anomalous billing patterns before claims are paid out."

## Market & Competition

Competes against larger incumbents Cotiviti and HMS/Gainwell, both of which
under-serve mid-size payers with slower time-to-value; Solace's 6-week
implementation versus an industry-typical 4-6 months is a credible, specific
differentiator rather than a vague claim.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Solace differentiates on faster time-to-value (6 weeks vs. an industry-typical 4-6 months) and pricing aimed at mid-size payers these incumbents underserve."

## Team & Funding

$45M raised across seed through a $22M Series C (2024). CEO Priya Nair brings
9 years of directly relevant experience as a data science lead at a major
payer, a strong domain-fit signal for this specific product category.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "CEO Priya Nair (co-founder, ex-UnitedHealth data science lead, 9 years)."

## Technology & IP

Three issued (not merely pending) patents on the core anomaly-detection
methods, plus SOC2 Type II and HIPAA compliance independently audited
annually — both real moat signals and table-stakes requirements for
handling payer claims data.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Three issued patents covering claims-anomaly-detection machine-learning methods (filed 2019-2021, granted 2021-2023). SOC2 Type II and HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, audited annually by a third party."

## Traction & Financials

$14M ARR growing 40% YoY across 28 payer customers, including 2 regional Blue
Cross Blue Shield plans as credible enterprise anchors. 118% net revenue
retention indicates the product expands within existing accounts, not just
land-and-stall.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "28 payer customers, including 2 regional Blue Cross Blue Shield plans. $14M ARR, growing 40% year over year. Net revenue retention is 118% over the trailing twelve months."

## Risks & Red Flags

Enterprise sales cycles run 9-14 months, which limits near-term revenue
predictability, and roughly 35% of new pipeline depends on two
systems-integrator partners rather than a fully direct motion.

> [solace-overview] Solace Health Analytics Company Overview — company-overview.md: "Two systems-integrator partners (regional health-IT consultancies) source roughly 35% of new pipeline; the remainder is direct enterprise sales."

## Open Questions

- What is the retention/expansion trend specifically among the two BCBS anchor accounts?
- How dependent is the 40% YoY growth rate on the two SI partners renewing their referral relationships?
