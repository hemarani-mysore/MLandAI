# Solace Health Analytics — Company Overview

Solace Health Analytics builds claims-analytics SaaS for mid-size health
insurance payers, focused on detecting anomalous billing patterns before
claims are paid out.

**Company facts.** Founded 2017, Minneapolis MN. Raised a $45M total across
seed through Series C (latest: $22M Series C, closed 2024). 110 employees, 38
in engineering/data science.

**Traction.** 28 payer customers, including 2 regional Blue Cross Blue Shield
plans. $14M ARR, growing 40% year over year. Net revenue retention is 118%
over the trailing twelve months, driven by upsells into adjacent
fraud-detection modules.

**Product & IP.** Three issued patents covering claims-anomaly-detection
machine-learning methods (filed 2019-2021, granted 2021-2023). SOC2 Type II
and HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, audited annually by a third party.

**Market & competition.** Competes with Cotiviti and HMS (now part of Gainwell)
for payment-integrity workloads, both larger incumbents with broader but less
specialized product suites; Solace differentiates on faster time-to-value (6
weeks vs. an industry-typical 4-6 months) and pricing aimed at mid-size payers
these incumbents underserve.

**Sales motion.** Enterprise sales cycles run 9-14 months from first contact
to signed contract, typical for payer procurement. Two systems-integrator
partners (regional health-IT consultancies) source roughly 35% of new
pipeline; the remainder is direct enterprise sales.

**Team.** CEO Priya Nair (co-founder, ex-UnitedHealth data science lead, 9
years); CTO reports directly to the board on security posture given the
HIPAA-regulated data the platform handles.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** Long sales cycles limit near-term
revenue predictability; meaningful pipeline dependency on two
systems-integrator partners rather than a fully direct motion.
