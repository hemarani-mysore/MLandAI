# Due-Diligence Memo — Meridian Cyber

**Recommendation:** `proceed_with_conditions`  ·  **Confidence:** 72%

## Summary

Meridian is a Series B SIEM/threat-detection vendor with strong growth (55%
YoY), healthy retention (108% NRR), and real differentiation against larger
incumbents in the mid-market segment. Customer concentration should be
monitored going forward as a condition, though current levels are not a
near-term concern.

## Overview

Meridian Cyber (founded 2019) builds SIEM/threat-detection software for
mid-market enterprises, a segment it argues is underserved by
enterprise-focused vendors.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "Meridian Cyber builds SIEM/threat-detection software for mid-market enterprises (500-5,000 employees), a segment it argues is underserved by enterprise-focused vendors."

## Market & Competition

Competes against CrowdStrike and SentinelOne, both of which focus primarily on
large enterprise accounts; Meridian's mid-market pricing and white-glove
onboarding is a credible differentiator against vendors with largely
self-service models at that segment.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "Meridian differentiates on mid-market pricing and white-glove onboarding support, a segment it claims these larger vendors serve poorly with self-service-only models."

## Team & Funding

$32M Series B raised. Both the CEO and VP of Engineering bring direct,
relevant prior experience running a Fortune-500-scale security operations
center.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "CEO and VP of Engineering both previously worked at a Fortune-500-scale security operations center before founding Meridian."

## Technology & IP

Four patents pending on anomaly-detection ML methods, plus SOC2 Type II and
ISO 27001 certification — solid baseline trust signals for a security
product, though the patents are still pending rather than issued.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "Four patents pending on anomaly-detection machine-learning methods. SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified."

## Traction & Financials

$11M ARR growing 55% YoY with 108% net revenue retention — healthy expansion
alongside new-logo growth, consistent with a product mid-market customers are
adopting and expanding usage of over time.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "$11M ARR, growing 55% year over year. Net revenue retention is 108% over the trailing twelve months."

## Risks & Red Flags

Top 5 customers represent 22% of ARR. At this stage and with 85 employees
across presumably dozens of total customers implied by an $11M ARR base, this
is a moderate but not alarming concentration level; it is worth monitoring as
the company scales rather than treating as disqualifying today.

> [meridian-overview] Meridian Cyber Company Overview — overview.md: "Top 5 customers represent 22% of ARR — a level the company characterizes as healthy diversification for its stage."

## Open Questions

- How does the patent-pending portfolio's scope compare to CrowdStrike/SentinelOne's issued patents in the same space?
- What is the trend in top-5 customer concentration over the last four quarters, not just the current snapshot?
