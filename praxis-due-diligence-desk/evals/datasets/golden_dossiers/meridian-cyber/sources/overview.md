# Meridian Cyber — Company Overview

Meridian Cyber builds SIEM/threat-detection software for mid-market
enterprises (500-5,000 employees), a segment it argues is underserved by
enterprise-focused vendors.

**Company facts.** Founded 2019. Raised a $32M Series B. 85 employees.

**Traction.** $11M ARR, growing 55% year over year. Net revenue retention is
108% over the trailing twelve months. Top 5 customers represent 22% of ARR —
a level the company characterizes as healthy diversification for its stage.

**Product & IP.** Four patents pending on anomaly-detection machine-learning
methods. SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.

**Market & competition.** Competes against CrowdStrike and SentinelOne, both
of which focus primarily on large enterprise accounts; Meridian differentiates
on mid-market pricing and white-glove onboarding support, a segment it claims
these larger vendors serve poorly with self-service-only models.

**Team.** CEO and VP of Engineering both previously worked at a
Fortune-500-scale security operations center before founding Meridian.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** Customer concentration in the top 5
accounts (22% of ARR) should be monitored as the company scales, though
current levels are not considered a near-term concern.
