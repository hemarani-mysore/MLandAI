# Due-Diligence Memo — OrbitFleet Logistics

**Recommendation:** `proceed_with_conditions`  ·  **Confidence:** 70%

## Summary

OrbitFleet is a Series B route-optimization SaaS vendor for regional trucking
fleets, with real traction (120 customers, $6.2M ARR, 65% YoY growth, 92%
gross retention) and a credible, benchmarked value proposition against larger
incumbents who under-serve its segment. The main condition to proceed is
validating that its lead pipeline can diversify beyond a small number of
industry conferences.

## Overview

OrbitFleet Logistics (founded 2019, Austin TX) sells route-optimization
software to regional trucking fleets of 50-500 trucks, integrating with
existing telematics rather than replacing it.

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "OrbitFleet Logistics builds route-optimization SaaS for regional trucking fleets (50-500 trucks), plugging into existing telematics and fuel-card systems rather than replacing them."

## Market & Competition

Direct competitors (Samsara, Motive, Fleetio) target larger national fleets or
treat routing as a minor feature; OrbitFleet's regional-fleet focus is a real
differentiator, backed by its own 30-fleet benchmark showing 15-20% fuel-cost
reduction versus manual dispatch.

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "OrbitFleet positions specifically for the 50-500-truck regional segment, where it claims a 15-20% fuel-cost reduction versus manual dispatch, based on its own customer benchmarking across 30 fleets."

## Team & Funding

$18M Series B raised in early 2025 (on top of $4M seed + $6M Series A). CEO
Maria Delgado is a first-time founder but has direct domain experience as a
former logistics-ops manager; CTO Sam Okafor brings 6 years at a
direct competitor (Samsara).

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "CEO Maria Delgado (first-time founder, ex-logistics-ops manager at a regional carrier); CTO Sam Okafor (ex-Samsara senior engineer, 6 years)."

## Technology & IP

One patent pending on a dynamic multi-stop routing algorithm, two more
provisional filings in process. Integrates with the three major telematics
platforms (Samsara, Motive, Geotab), reducing switching friction for
prospects already on those systems.

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "OrbitFleet has one patent pending (filed Q3 2024) on a dynamic multi-stop routing algorithm that re-optimizes routes in response to live traffic and load changes."

## Traction & Financials

$6.2M ARR growing 65% YoY across 120 customers, 92% gross logo retention, and
rising average contract value ($38K → $52K) as existing customers expand
usage — a healthy expansion signal on top of new-logo growth.

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "120 fleet customers, $6.2M ARR, growing 65% year over year. Gross logo retention is 92% over the trailing twelve months."

## Risks & Red Flags

The company's own pipeline data shows roughly 55% of new-logo leads over the
last two quarters came from just three industry conferences — a concentrated,
somewhat seasonal channel that has not yet been proven to diversify into
scalable outbound or partner-led motion. The first-time CEO risk is partially
mitigated by an experienced COO hire.

> [orbitfleet-pitch-deck] OrbitFleet Company Overview — pitch-deck.md: "~55% of new-logo pipeline in the last two quarters originated from three regional trucking-industry conferences."

## Open Questions

- What does the outbound/channel pipeline look like excluding conference-sourced leads?
- How defensible is the routing algorithm once the two additional patents are examined, not just filed?
