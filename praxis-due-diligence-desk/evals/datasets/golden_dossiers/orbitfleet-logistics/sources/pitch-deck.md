# OrbitFleet Logistics — Company Overview

OrbitFleet Logistics builds route-optimization SaaS for regional trucking
fleets (50-500 trucks), plugging into existing telematics and fuel-card
systems rather than replacing them.

**Company facts.** Founded 2019, Austin TX. Raised a $18M Series B in early
2025 (prior: $4M seed, $6M Series A). 40 employees, 14 in engineering.

**Traction.** 120 fleet customers, $6.2M ARR, growing 65% year over year.
Gross logo retention is 92% over the trailing twelve months. Average contract
value has grown from $38K to $52K as customers add more trucks to the
platform.

**Product & IP.** OrbitFleet has one patent pending (filed Q3 2024) on a
dynamic multi-stop routing algorithm that re-optimizes routes in response to
live traffic and load changes; two more provisional filings are in process.
The product integrates with Samsara, Motive, and Geotab telematics feeds.

**Market & competition.** Direct competitors — Samsara, Motive, and Fleetio —
primarily target large national fleets (1,000+ trucks) or offer routing as a
minor feature of a broader telematics suite. OrbitFleet positions specifically
for the 50-500-truck regional segment, where it claims a 15-20% fuel-cost
reduction versus manual dispatch, based on its own customer benchmarking
across 30 fleets.

**Team.** CEO Maria Delgado (first-time founder, ex-logistics-ops manager at a
regional carrier); CTO Sam Okafor (ex-Samsara senior engineer, 6 years).
Board includes two Series A/B investors.

**Go-to-market.** ~55% of new-logo pipeline in the last two quarters
originated from three regional trucking-industry conferences (TCA Annual,
Manifest, Truckload Carriers Association regional events); the remainder from
outbound sales and channel referrals from telematics partners.

**Risks flagged by the company itself.** Heavy reliance on a small number of
industry conferences for top-of-funnel; CEO's first time in the CEO role
(mitigated by an experienced COO hire in Q1 2025).
